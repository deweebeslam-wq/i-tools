Pipe Splitter

Releasedi-Tools for Revit

Split, trim or cut pieces out of pipes at levels ± offset, elevations, positions or regular spacing — with a preview and one Undo.

What it does

Pick pipes, use the Revit selection or take every pipe the active view shows; filter by direction (vertical, horizontal, sloped — from the real 3D line), system, size and level. Split at levels ± an offset or typed elevations, keep or delete what lies above / below / between, cut at typed positions, or every X / into N equal pieces. Every cut and every deleted piece is previewed per pipe before anything changes; kept cuts are joined with the pipe type's union (or connected directly), the original element always survives, a pipe that cannot be split safely is left exactly as it was, and the whole run is one Undo.
